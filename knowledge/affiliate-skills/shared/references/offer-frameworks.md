# Ethical offer design reference

This document is original project guidance informed by the supplied affiliate marketing materials. It is doctrine for content generation, not proof that a merchant will deliver a bonus, guarantee, price, feature, or result.

## Evidence before persuasion

For every offer, distinguish verified product facts, publisher-created deliverables, opinions, assumptions, and unknown information. Product claims require a current primary source or must be marked unverified. Never fabricate testimonials, earnings claims, scarcity, countdowns, results, or discounts.

## Offer design checklist

1. Audience and use case: state who may benefit and who likely will not.
2. Desired outcome: describe the problem the product is designed to address without promising a guaranteed result.
3. Evidence: link the current product page, terms, pricing, and official feature/limitations documentation.
4. Time and effort: state setup or learning requirements only when substantiated.
5. Alternatives: identify relevant lower-cost, free, or non-product alternatives.
6. Bonuses: include only assets the publisher actually owns and can deliver, with clear eligibility and fulfillment rules.
7. Guarantee and refund: quote merchant policy accurately; never imply the publisher can alter it.
8. CTA: describe the next step and any material conditions.
9. Disclosure: place a clear affiliate disclosure near the recommendation and any relevant CTA.
10. Review: preserve evidence timestamps and obtain required human approval before external publication.

## Value ladder boundaries

Free content must provide useful standalone value. Optional starter, core, and premium steps are planning concepts, not permission to invent products or prices. Do not confuse the merchant's product price, the affiliate commission, publisher-created services, and verified customer outcomes.

## Ethical urgency

Only communicate a deadline or scarcity when a current, authoritative source proves it. Otherwise omit urgency language. Never fabricate a perceived value for non-existent bonuses or claim personal testing that did not occur.
