# S1–S8 flywheel data contracts

- **S1 Research:** demand inputs, public source observations, offer terms, provider evidence and uncertainty.
- **S2 Content intelligence:** source collection, claim ledger, audience angle and platform-native content briefs.
- **S3 SEO:** intent mapping, keyword clusters, helpful articles, metadata, internal-link suggestions and freshness observations.
- **S4 Offers and landing:** verified offer facts, actual deliverables, CTA, disclosure, privacy notice and consent capture.
- **S5 Distribution:** authorized channel, approved asset, scheduled job, idempotency key, remote ID and policy gate.
- **S6 Measurement:** click IDs, UTMs, attributed conversion events, commission states and experiment assignments.
- **S7 Operations:** budget reservations, retry policy, worker health, kill switches and audit events.
- **S8 Learning:** only confirmed/adequately sampled outcomes become hypotheses; preserve evidence, uncertainty and rollback.

A completed stage means its documented operation finished; it does not prove sales, provider approval, profit, or external publication. Stages must receive typed data and evidence from prior stages, must fail visibly when prerequisites are missing, and must not fabricate values to advance the flywheel.
