# Affiliate marketing glossary

- **Affiliate / publisher:** a person or entity promoting a merchant's product under program terms.
- **Merchant / advertiser:** a party offering a product or service and governing its affiliate relationship.
- **Affiliate network:** intermediary that provides program discovery, tracking, reporting, or settlement services.
- **CPA/CPL/CPC:** cost per action, lead, or click; definitions vary by provider contract.
- **Commission:** amount reported as owed under the program, subject to pending, approved, reversed, hold, and payout states.
- **EPC:** commission or revenue divided by a defined click population and time window. Do not calculate from missing denominators.
- **Cookie / attribution window:** provider-defined period and rules for assigning conversion credit.
- **Sub-ID / click ID:** tracking value used to connect a click to later events, subject to provider policies and privacy constraints.
- **Reversal / chargeback / refund:** events that may reverse or adjust an earlier commission observation; do not silently delete earlier events.
- **Payout threshold:** a program's conditions for payment processing; reaching a threshold is not proof money was received.
- **Affiliate disclosure:** clear notice of a material connection between the publisher and a recommended product.
- **Terms version:** timestamped record of the terms the account accepted or relied upon.
