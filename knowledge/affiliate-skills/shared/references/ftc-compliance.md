# Affiliate disclosure and consumer-protection checklist

Affiliate disclosures and consumer-protection laws vary by jurisdiction and change over time. Check current official guidance and obtain legal review for the intended markets. This checklist is product guidance, not legal advice.

- Make the material connection clear, understandable, and near the recommendation/link.
- Use plain language (for example: “I may earn a commission if you buy through this link”) only when that wording accurately describes the relationship.
- Do not assume a profile bio, footer, or generic terms page makes every individual endorsement clear.
- Do not make unsupported performance, medical, financial, safety, earnings, or comparative claims.
- Disclose material limitations, incentives, sponsorships, gifts, or relationships where required.
- Preserve source evidence for product claims and record approval/review history.
- Provide an unsubscribe mechanism in marketing email and honor suppression promptly.
- Do not collect or reuse leads without clear purpose, consent where required, privacy notice, and retention controls.
